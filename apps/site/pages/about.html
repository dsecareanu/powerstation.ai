About and sources

powerstation.ai is a free, open-source tool. It shows how electricity moves across Europe, hour by hour. You can also test a question such as "What happens to the grid if a 1 GW data center goes here?"

Two steps

  1. Watch the grid: a map of electricity flows between European countries, for the last 7 or 30 days.
  2. Place a data center: pick a grid zone and a size. See how the extra demand compares with that zone's load, imports and electricity mix.

How to read the numbers

Every value on this site has one of four labels:

Measured
Reported by the grid operators after the hour.
Planned
A plan made the day before, for example planned trade or planned transfer capacity between zones. A plan is not a forecast of what will physically flow.
Modelled
Our estimate. The method is shown next to the value.
No data
The value is missing. We do not fill the gap.

Power is in megawatts (MW), as the average over one hour. Times are stored in UTC and shown in your local time. If the newest data is more than 2 hours old, the page says "data delayed".

The calculator gives an estimate for a whole grid zone. It cannot judge a specific site, a local grid connection or a permit.
